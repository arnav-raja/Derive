<Callback lesson="E1.2">

`range(1, 5)` gives 1, 2, 3 and 4, stopping before 5. The total starts at 0, and each pass adds the square of `x`: 1, then $1 + 4 = 5$, then $5 + 9 = 14$, then $14 + 16 = 30$. That is the accumulator pattern: set the total before the loop, and update it on every pass.
