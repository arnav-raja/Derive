## How far a buffer moves an item

Number the items of the stream from 0, in the order they arrive, and the items that `shuffled` hands out from 0 too, in the order they leave. With a buffer of $k$, nothing is handed out until the buffer is full. Then item $k$ arrives, and output 0 is chosen from the buffer; item $k + 1$ arrives, and output 1 is chosen; and so on. So output $j$ is chosen when item $k + j$ arrives, from the items in the buffer, all of which arrived before it.

So a buffer of $k$ moves an item forward by at most $k - 1$ places. It can move an item back by any number of places, since the random choice may pass over it again and again. With a buffer of 1,000, the item at place 50,000 cannot come out before place 49,001, however the choices fall, which is why the Explore phase's sorted file stays nearly sorted.
