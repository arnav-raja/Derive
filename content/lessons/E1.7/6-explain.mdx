## Where this leads

From here on, almost every value in the course is an object of some class: a tensor, a layer, a model, an optimiser, a dataset.

<Foreshadow lesson="M6.5">

An expression worked out with objects whose `+` and `*` remember their inputs leaves behind its whole tree, as the Derive phase's `Value` did. M6.5 calls that tree a computational graph, and finds every derivative of the result by walking it backwards, once.

</Foreshadow>

<Foreshadow lesson="L3.4">

The Build phase's `Value` keeps a number and the values it was made from. L3.4 gives it a gradient and a backward step, and builds from it an autograd engine that trains a small network.

</Foreshadow>

<Foreshadow lesson="E5.4">

Every layer of a PyTorch model is a subclass of `Module`, whose `__call__` does some bookkeeping and then runs the layer's own `forward`, as the Explore phase's `Layer` did. E5.4 looks at modules, the parameters they own, the state they keep and the hooks that run around `forward`.

</Foreshadow>
