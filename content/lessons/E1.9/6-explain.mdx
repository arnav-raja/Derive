## Where this leads

Induction, which M1.9 teaches, proves a claim about every whole number with two finite arguments, a base case and a step. A property makes the same kind of claim about every input, but a test checks it only on the inputs it tries: one input that fails disproves the claim, and any number that pass prove nothing. Where a proof is possible, it settles what testing can only sample. Where it is not, a property checked on thousands of well-chosen inputs, and every failure shrunk and kept as a test of its own, is the next best thing.
