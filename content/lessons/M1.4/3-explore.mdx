The puzzle's squared error is a curve with a lowest point. Before finding that point exactly, look at how the outputs of simple rules change as their inputs change, starting with the simplest kind of change.

## Steady change

A cloud company rents out a GPU for ₹150 an hour, plus a one-off ₹200 to set the machine up. After $t$ hours the bill is $C(t) = 150t + 200$ rupees.

| Hours $t$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bill $C(t)$ in ₹ | 200 | 350 | 500 | 650 | 800 |

Each extra hour adds ₹150, whichever hour it is. So between any two times, the change in the bill divided by the change in time comes out the same. From 1 hour to 4 hours the bill rises by ₹450 over 3 hours, which is ₹150 per hour. That ratio is the *rate of change* of the bill. As M1.1, on quantities and units, showed, units divide along with the numbers, so the rate is measured in rupees per hour.

Running the clock back from 2 seconds to 0 takes away $2 \times 15 = 30$ MB, so 10 MB had already arrived when the clock started, and the amount after $t$ seconds is $A(t) = 15t + 10$. The download and the GPU bill are the same kind of rule: a starting value, plus a fixed rate times the input. A rate can also be negative. A tank that loses 4 litres every minute changes at $-4$ litres per minute, and its line slopes down instead of up.

## Change that changes

The squares rise faster and faster. Here are more of them, with the rise from each one to the next:

| $x$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $x^2$ | 0 | 1 | 4 | 9 | 16 | 25 |
| Rise from the one before | | 1 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 9 |

For such a rule the rate depends on where you measure it. Between two different inputs $u$ and $v$, the rise divided by the step, $\dfrac{v^2 - u^2}{v - u}$, is called the *average rate of change* from $u$ to $v$. On the graph it is the *slope* of the straight line that joins the two points, a line called a *chord*: how far the chord rises for every unit it goes across.

## The bottom of a bowl

Every rule built from $x^2$, $x$ and numbers, with a number other than 0 in front of $x^2$, draws the same kind of curve: a bowl, or an upturned bowl when that number is negative. Here is $y = (x - 2)^2 + 1$.

This formula shows where the bottom is. A square is never negative, so $(x - 2)^2$ is 0 when $x = 2$ and more than 0 everywhere else. The lowest value of $y$ is therefore 1, at $x = 2$.

Going a distance $d$ either way from 2 gives the same height, $d^2 + 1$, because $d$ and $-d$ have the same square. So the bowl is symmetric: points of equal height come in pairs, mirrored about the line $x = 2$, and the bottom is halfway between the two points of any pair.

## Finding the bottom without a picture

The bowl $y = x^2 - 6x + 11$ has a formula that does not show its bottom. The mirror gives a way to find it anyway: find two inputs with the same height, and the bottom is halfway between them.

<Exercise id="explore-find-bottom" />

</Reveal>

<Reveal for="explore-find-bottom">

The heights at $x = 0$ and $x = 6$ agree because $x^2 - 6x = x(x - 6)$ is 0 at both. At the bottom, $x = 3$, the height is $9 - 18 + 11 = 2$.

There is a second way to see it. Multiply out $(x - 3)^2 + 2$: it is $x^2 - 6x + 9 + 2 = x^2 - 6x + 11$. So the formula was $(x - 3)^2 + 2$ all along, written out, and in that form it shows its bottom at once, just as $(x - 2)^2 + 1$ did. Rewriting a quadratic as a squared bracket plus a number is called *completing the square*, and the Derive phase does it for every quadratic, including the puzzle's.

## The names

<Definition term="Linear function">

A function of the form $f(x) = mx + c$, where $m$ and $c$ are fixed numbers. Its graph is a straight line, and it changes at the same rate everywhere.

</Definition>

<Definition term="Slope and intercept">

In $f(x) = mx + c$, the number $m$ is the *slope*: the rate of change, the same between any two points, measured in units of output per unit of input. The number $c$ is the *intercept*: the output at $x = 0$, where the line crosses the vertical axis.

</Definition>

<Definition term="Average rate of change">

The average rate of change of $f$ from $u$ to $v$, for two different inputs, is $\dfrac{f(v) - f(u)}{v - u}$: the change in output divided by the change in input. It is the slope of the chord that joins the two points of the graph. For a linear function it is the slope $m$, whichever two points you choose.

</Definition>

<Definition term="Quadratic function">

A function of the form $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$ with $a \ne 0$. Its graph is a *parabola*: a bowl that opens upwards when $a > 0$ and downwards when $a < 0$.

</Definition>

<Definition term="Vertex">

The lowest point of a parabola that opens upwards, or the highest point of one that opens downwards. The parabola is symmetric about the vertical line through its vertex.

</Definition>
